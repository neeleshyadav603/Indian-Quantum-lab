Hi, I'm Arjun Mehta

Quantum Researcher, Educator, Published Author

I'm a Quantum Machine Learning researcher based in Gurugram, India, specializing in hybrid quantum-classical algorithms, variational quantum circuits, and NISQ-era applications. My work focuses on developing practical quantum solutions for finance, healthcare, and real-world machine learning problems while contributing to quantum education and open research.

I founded Indian Quantum Lab to make high-quality quantum computing education freely accessible through structured learning resources, open-source projects, research-driven tutorials, and hands-on Qiskit applications. My mission is to help students, researchers, and developers build practical quantum computing skills regardless of their academic background.

Our Mission

Making quantum computing education accessible to every Indian student — regardless of their college, city, or background — because the quantum revolution needs all of India's talent.

The Story

In 2022, I was a second-year physics PhD student at IISc Bangalore, desperately trying to understand quantum error correction. The textbooks were dense, the research papers assumed graduate-level physics, and the few online courses that existed cost $500+ — a significant sum for a PhD stipend.

I started a Telegram group with 20 other struggling students. We shared notes, solved problems together, and slowly, things clicked. That group grew to 500 students within a year, all across India — from IITs to tier-3 engineering colleges, from metros to small towns.

Today, we've grown to 2,400+ Discord members, 15,000+ monthly blog readers, and partnerships with 12 engineering colleges. But the mission remains the same: free forever, open to everyone, made in India.
